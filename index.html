Statewide primary vote (%)?

Primary vote is the share of first-choice (number 1) votes for each party across Victoria. This is the figure polls usually report first.

Where One Nation's vote lands?

One Nation barely ran in 2022, so there is no local history to build on. This slider sets how much stronger its vote is in regional Victoria than in inner Melbourne. These weights are estimates.

0 = same in every seat · 1 = stronger in regional Victoria, weaker in inner Melbourne (default) · 2 = strongly regional

Preference flows (%)?

Victoria uses preferential voting. When a party is knocked out of the count, its votes pass to the voters' next choices. ONP → Coal. 75 means 75% of One Nation votes go to the Coalition ahead of Labor.

About this tool: An independent scenario explorer, not a forecast. It shows what might happen if a poll were correct on election day, using 2022 results and simplified assumptions about swings and preferences. Real results depend on local candidates, independents and campaigns. Poll figures are as published and not endorsed by the pollsters. Not affiliated with any political party, candidate, pollster or the Victorian Electoral Commission. For official information, visit vec.vic.gov.au.
